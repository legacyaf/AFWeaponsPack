WW2-style bolt-action sniper rifle. Second weapon of the pack, and the first one built by following `docs/PIPELINE.md`.

## Look

Full-length walnut stock with a straight wrist and finger grooves, upper handguard, blued barrel and fittings, bright bolt with a turned-down handle, tangent rear sight, eared front sight, cleaning rod, bayonet lug, steel butt plate. A dark steel disc with the orange AF logo is let into both sides of the butt.

## Bones

The Mosin skeleton has nine bones. The model uses five. The vanilla bolt is split over two bones only: `boltbody` (467 triangles, the rear of the bolt) and `bolthandle` (583 triangles, the handle and the part that turns).

| Bone | Part | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| `trigger` | trigger | name is unambiguous |
| `bolthandle` | handle and knob | name is unambiguous |
| `boltbody` | bolt and cocking piece | name is unambiguous |
| `boltbodys` | nothing | 0.8.0-0.8.3 had the cocking piece here by guess; in game it lagged behind the bolt when firing. The vanilla model has no geometry on this bone (decoded 0.8.4), so the cocking piece moved to `boltbody`. |

| `bullet1` | top round in the magazine | read from the vanilla model |
| `bullet` | second round in the magazine | read from the vanilla model |

The vanilla Mosin model was decoded to check this (0.8.1). It carries two display cartridges, 167 triangles each, in the selections `bullet` and `bullet1`; they lie in the magazine under the bolt and the loading animation moves them. The M41's two rounds sit at the same positions and have the same length:

| Round | Case head (y, z) | Bullet tip (y, z) |
|---|---|---|
| `bullet1` (top, nose up) | -0.0036, 0.1058 | 0.0611, 0.1091 |
| `bullet` | -0.0025, 0.0903 | 0.0623, 0.0903 |

The vanilla weapon model has **no stripper clip** in it and nothing on `magazine`, `rotation_a` or `rotation_b`, so the M41 has nothing there either. The Mosin's config does not list `bullet` in `simpleHiddenSelections`, so the rounds are shown and hidden by the animation alone; the M41 inherits that unchanged.

To make the rounds visible, the stock and the receiver floor have a magazine well cut through them, lined with a steel box.

## Ghillie wrap and sling (0.8.3)

The vanilla Mosin carries three proxies of `dz\weapons\attachments\support\ghillie_wrap` (slot `weaponWrap`). The M41 has the same three, at the vanilla positions and orientations, in the same order:

| Proxy | Where | Working-frame position (y, z) |
|---|---|---|
| `.001` | receiver | 0.1280, 0.1385 |
| `.002` | handguard, turned round | 0.5108, 0.1385 |
| `.003` | butt, tilted 5.7 degrees | -0.2364, 0.1010 |

The export script numbers proxies of the same model in the order they appear in the `PROXIES` table.

The sling is a static leather strap from the band swivel to the butt swivel, hanging with 1.1 cm of slack, with a keeper loop at each end. It does not move. Seen in game on 0.8.3: it clears both hands and the trigger guard but crosses the left forearm when aiming; the owner chose to leave it hanging (2026-11-18). It is part of `camo`, so the gold finish leaves it as leather.

## Status

Built, binarized and signed. Checked outside the game: `config.cpp` compiles, Object Builder's script engine loads all twelve LODs with the right selections, 4 kg mass and convex collision, the binarized model carries the same nine-bone skeleton as the vanilla Mosin, and the signature verifies.

**Not yet tested in game.** The checklist is in `docs/TESTING.md` (steps M1-M7). First things to check, in order:

1. Bolt cycle: handle lifts and the bolt slides back as one piece. If the cocking piece stays behind or moves oddly, `boltbodys` is the wrong bone for it; move that part to `boltbody` in `afm41_pathfinder_dayz_export.py`.
2. Hand positions on the stock and bolt knob.
3. Iron sight picture: front blade centred in the rear notch.
4. PU scope seating on the receiver.
5. Compensator, suppressor and bayonet positions at the muzzle.
6. Wood colour and shine in daylight.

## Not done yet

- The two magazine rounds (0.8.1) are untested in game: check that they are hidden when the rifle is empty and move with the loading animation.
- Ghillie wrap slot and leather sling (0.8.3) are untested in game: check the three wrap pieces sit on the rifle, and that the sling does not cut through the hands.
- The gold finish has only been previewed in Blender, where the steel reads grey; in game it should be black, as on the AF-7 Gold.
- Handguard and stock are simple rounded sections; no checkering.
