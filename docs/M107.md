# AF M107A1 (.50 BMG anti-materiel rifle): design

Owner's choice (2026-11-23): "something unique, the latest model of Barrett 50 cal released by the US military". The US Army's current .50 rifle is the M107 family; the M107A1 is its lighter, suppressor-ready successor. No newer .50 BMG Barrett adoption was found (searched 2026-11-23).

Name in game: **AF M107A1**. Barrett is a trademark, so the brand name is not used in the mod; the military designation is.

## Measured facts (manufacturer's documents)

Construction, from the manual's parts list and assembly steps:

- **Upper receiver.** A long sheet-steel box carrying:
  - the top rail and the cheek piece (3 screws);
  - the rear sight (elevation screw on top, windage knob on the left);
  - the folding front sight (front sight, catch and spring);
  - the barrel springs at the front, held by the barrel key and the front barrel bushing;
  - the front sling loop.
  
  The barrel nests inside it for takedown.
- **Lower receiver.** Integral with the stock, in steel. It carries:
  - the pistol grip, trigger and thumb safety (left, above the grip);
  - the magazine catch;
  - the cocking lever (charging handle, right);
  - the bipod yoke mount;
  - the rear grip, a rotating monopod foot with an elevation screw and lock knob, and the recoil pad (2 screws);
  - a hydraulic recoil buffer and mainspring inside.
- **Joining the halves.** The upper and lower receivers are held together by a mid lock pin and a rear lock pin, both with finger rings.
- **Bipod.** A yoke with two legs. Each leg locks forward, rearward or at 90 degrees, and extends; the feet have shoes.
- **Muzzle brake.** Cylindrical, with side ports, fixed by a screw, and accepts the QDL suppressor.
- **Finishes.** Black, Tungsten Grey and Flat Dark Earth Cerakote.

**Not in the documents:** overall height, receiver section sizes, brake length and diameter, bipod leg length, and magazine size. These need reference photos (owner) and are measured from them, scaled to the 1443 mm overall length.

## DayZ integration (plan)

- **Handling:** inherits the vanilla SVD (semi-automatic, detachable 10-round magazine, long rifle animations).
- **Ammunition:** its own .50 BMG round and box, `Ammo_AF50BMG` (no vanilla .50 exists). Damage is well above the 7.62x54R. The server settings file (`AF_WeaponsPack.json`, `DamageMultiplier`) lets owners tone it down.
- **Magazine:** its own, `Mag_AFM107_10Rnd`.
- **Optics:** the top rail takes the vanilla long-range scopes in the usual optics slot. Its own scope can follow later.
- **Bipod:** modelled (legs folded along the barrel). DayZ has no deployable bipod for these animations, so it is visual.
- **Finishes:** the three manufacturer colours as variants (Black, Tungsten Grey, Flat Dark Earth). Cerakote is a sprayed ceramic coating: matte, a slight sheen, edges worn to grey steel.
- **Built with the StG 44's methods from the start:**
  - high-to-low bake;
  - wear placed by use (cheek piece, rear grip, charging handle, magazine well, bipod feet, muzzle brake);
  - specular and gloss spread checked against a vanilla rifle;
  - edge padding;
  - an in-game lighting series.
