0.12.0

Two new weapons: the AF StG 44 and the AF M107A1.

New weapon: AF StG 44
- Assault rifle in the pattern of 1944: stamped steel receiver, beech stock, long curved magazine.
- Fires the vanilla 7.62x39mm rounds from its own 30-round magazine (a new item). Single shots or automatic, at a steady rate of about 570 rounds a minute.
- A third less recoil than the AKM.
- Takes its own AF ZF4 scope (a new item, 1.5x, fits no other weapon), AK muzzle devices, the improvised suppressor and a ghillie wrap.
- Two finishes: beech and steel, or gold floral on black.
- Uses the AKM animations and sounds.
- Built with a new finishing method:
  - surface detail baked from a far more detailed model of the rifle;
  - wear placed where hands, sling and kit really rub;
  - pressed-steel detail: flutes, spot welds, markings;
  - real beech grain;
  - steel shine matched to the vanilla AKM.
  Checked in game in noon sun, overcast, evening and morning light.

New weapon: AF M107A1
- .50 BMG anti-materiel rifle: recoil-operated, semi-automatic, 10-round magazine. Heavy (12.5 kg), long and loud.
- Fires its own .50 BMG rounds (a new item) from its own 10-round steel magazine (a new item).
- Built-in 6-12x scope with an illuminated red reticle, a zoom readout and a live rangefinder. The view through it is a round picture, black around the edges. Zeroing from 100 to 1,200 m.
- The scope is part of the rifle and cannot be taken off.
- Round handguard with lightening holes, full-length top rail, ribbed cheek plates, two-chamber muzzle brake and fluted barrel.
- Dark Cerakote finish with edge wear, grime in the corners, rivets, swage lines and its own roll mark.
- Uses the SVD animations, with a little more recoil than the SVD.

Server owners
- New class names: AF_StG44, AF_StG44_Gold, Mag_AFStG44_30Rnd, AF_ZF4Optic, AF_M107A1, Mag_AFM107_10Rnd, Ammo_AF50BMG.
- The loot files in ServerFiles have entries for all of them. Merge them again, or they will not spawn. Neither rifle takes any other magazine, and the M107A1 fires no vanilla round.
- The M107A1 is very rare by default: 2 on the map, Military, Tier 4.
- AF_WeaponsPack.json picks up both new weapons by itself on the next start. Recoil, damage and the other settings work for them as for the rest of the pack.
- No class name or loot changes for the AF-7, M41, MP40 or P08.
