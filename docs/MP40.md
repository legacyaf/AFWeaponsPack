# AF MP40

WW2-style 9 mm submachine gun. Third weapon of the pack, the first with its own magazine, and the first built on a vanilla weapon whose whole body sits on a bone.

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | `AF_MP40` (bakelite), `AF_MP40_Gold` (gold floral on black furniture), `Mag_AFMP40_32Rnd` (magazine) |
| Pack version | 0.10.0 |
| Size | gun LOD 1: 13,148 triangles; LODs 2-9: 8,676 / 5,784 / 3,811 / 2,423 / 1,536 / 1,001 / 564 / 301; two 4096 px texture sets (`afmp40_body` steel, `afmp40_furn` bakelite). Magazine LOD 1: 637 triangles, down to 13 at LOD 9; one 2048 px set (`afmp40_mag`) |
| Inherits | vanilla `UMP45` (animations, handling, recoil); the magazine inherits `Mag_UMP_25Rnd` |
| Different from the UMP | 9x19 mm, 32 rounds, automatic fire only at about 520 rounds a minute (`reloadTime` 0.115), MP5-K sound sets, slightly less recoil |
| Attachments | pistol suppressor, ghillie wrap. No optic or light: the gun has no rail. |
| Models | `source/AF_Weapons/afmp40/afmp40.p3d` and `afmp40_mag.p3d` |
| Blender | `blender/afmp40_detailed.py` (gun and magazine), `afmp40_dayz_export.py`, `afmp40_run.py` |

## Look

Steel receiver tube with a ribbed end cap, cocking slot with its safety notch on the left, ejection port on the top right with the bright bolt behind it, knurled barrel nut, slim barrel with a hooded front sight and a muzzle nut, bakelite resting bar under the barrel, flip rear sight, bakelite lower frame and grip panels on a steel grip frame, ribbed magazine housing with the release button on the left, under-folding stock drawn out with the butt plate swung down. The orange AF logo is on both sides of the lower frame.

## Frame and numbers taken from the vanilla UMP-45

The model is built in the UMP's own frame (see `docs/PIPELINE.md`, step 3).

| Vanilla feature | Working-frame value |
|---|---|
| Bore line | z = 0.0724 |
| Muzzle (UMP / MP40) | y = 0.2723 / y = 0.403 |
| Butt (UMP / MP40) | y = -0.42 / y = -0.39 |
| Trigger pivot | y = -0.0501, z = 0.0292 |
| Pistol grip | top y -0.115..-0.070, bottom y -0.150..-0.100 at z -0.073 |
| Magazine proxy (UMP) | y = 0.0708, z = -0.0385, leaning 13.7 degrees |
| Left hand | under the handguard, y 0.11..0.23, underside z = 0.045 |
| Eye point | y = -0.2741 (z = 0.1364 on the UMP, 0.0994 here: the MP40's own sight line) |

Two things were bent to fit that frame:

- The lower frame between the trigger guard and the magazine housing is about 4 cm shorter than on the real gun, so the trigger, the grip and the magazine are all where the UMP's animations put the hands.
- The resting bar starts right in front of the barrel nut, where the left hand closes when the gun is aimed.

## Bones

Read from the skeleton stored in the vanilla model: `magazine`, `trigger` and `recoil` have no parent; `bolt`, `charging` and `bullet` are children of `recoil`. On the vanilla model 4,221 of 4,797 triangles are on `recoil`: the whole body.

| Bone | Part |
|---|---|
| `recoil` | every part without a bone of its own (`ex.BODY_BONE`), and the suppressor and wrap proxies |
| `trigger` | trigger |
| `bolt` | bolt, seen through the ejection port |
| `charging` | cocking handle on the left |
| `magazine` | the magazine proxy |
| `bullet` | nothing |

On a real MP40 the cocking handle is fixed to the bolt and runs back and forth when firing. Here it is on `charging`, so it moves when the player cocks the gun and stays still when firing, as the UMP's does.

## Magazine

`Mag_AFMP40_32Rnd` is the only magazine the gun takes. Its model is built around its own origin (the middle of the magazine) and the gun's magazine proxy sits at y = 0.085, z = -0.0505, upright. The gun keeps the vanilla proxy path `magazine_ump45_25rnd`: the game ties that proxy name to the `magazine` slot and shows whichever magazine is attached.

The UMP's magazine leans 13.7 degrees; ours is upright and placed so that the two cross where the hand takes hold, about 5 cm below the housing.

## Status (0.10.0)

Checked outside the game: `config.cpp` and `model.cfg` compile, Object Builder's script engine loads all fourteen LODs of both models with the right selections, 4 kg and 0.35 kg mass and convex collision, the signature verifies.

Checked with the automated in-game test (`test\run_test.ps1 -Mission AFMP40Test.ChernarusPlus`):

- Both finishes, the gun without its magazine, the magazine alone, the damaged and ruined looks all render.
- Pistol suppressor and ghillie wrap fit; the muzzle nut hides under the suppressor.
- In the hands: right hand on the grip, left hand under the barrel nut and resting bar, magazine seated in the housing.
- Magazine swap: the game accepts it, and the hand takes the magazine out and puts the new one in.
- Inventory view and description.

## Not tested yet

- Firing: the bolt, the recoil kick and the sound. The test's scripted shot does not play the fire animation.
- Cocking: whether the cocking handle follows the hand.
- First-person view and the sight picture.
- Multiplayer and the licence check.

## Known compromises

- The magazine in the hand during a reload is upright while the UMP animation expects a leaning one; the hand may sit a little off the magazine.
- No sling loops, no folding of the stock.
