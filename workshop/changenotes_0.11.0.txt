0.11.0

New weapon: AF P08
- Toggle-lock 9 mm service pistol: slim tapered barrel, knurled toggle knobs, raked grip with checkered walnut panels.
- Fires 9x19mm from its own 8-round magazine (a new item).
- Takes a pistol suppressor or an improvised suppressor.
- Two finishes: walnut grips and blued steel, or gold floral grips on black.
- Uses the P1 animations and sounds. The toggle stays flat when the action opens.

New: server settings
- The server writes AF_WeaponsPack.json into its profiles folder on first start.
- For the whole pack and for each weapon: recoil, sway, damage and jam chance multipliers. Per weapon: switch it off, or add wear per shot. Optional: weapons take no damage from bullets.
- The settings only affect this pack's weapons, never vanilla or other mods' weapons.
- Every setting is explained in ServerFiles\SETTINGS.md.

New: AF Weapon Repair Kit
- Combine it with a weapon of the pack to repair it, even a ruined one.
- Repair time, amount restored, uses per kit and whether it works on suppressors are server settings.

New, switched off by default: a short sound when a weapon of the pack is aimed, zoomed or zeroed, heard by players nearby. Turn it on in the settings file.

All weapons
- Triggers are curved, rounded blades instead of flat cut-outs.

Server owners
- New class names: AF_P08, AF_P08_Gold, Mag_AFP08_8Rnd, AF_WeaponRepairKit.
- The loot files in ServerFiles have entries for all four. Merge them again, or they will not spawn. No other magazine fits the P08.
- No class name or loot changes for the AF-7, the M41 or the MP40.
