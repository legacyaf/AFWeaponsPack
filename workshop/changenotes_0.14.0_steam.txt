[h1]0.14.0: The PeaceKeeper[/h1]
A new weapon: the AF PeaceKeeper, a modern 7.62 battle rifle with a live round counter.

[h1]AF PeaceKeeper[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]7.62x51mm (.308 Win)[/b] from the vanilla 20-round SCAR-H magazines.
[*][b]Three fire modes:[/b] single shots, 3-round burst and full auto (about 610 rounds a minute).
[*][b]Live round counter[/b] on the side of the rifle. It shows the rounds left and glows, so it reads at night.
[*][b]Gold ghost-ring iron sights[/b] with a red front post tip.
[*][b]Its own AF stock.[/b] The vanilla SCAR stocks fit the rifle too, and the AF stock fits the vanilla SCAR-H.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] optic, flashlight, improvised suppressor and ghillie wrap.
[*][b]Finish:[/b] matte black with orange trim. Stippled grip, engraved markings and wear where the rifle is handled.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the SCAR-H animations and sounds.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AF_PeaceKeeper[/b] and the stock [b]AF_PeaceKeeperStock[/b]
