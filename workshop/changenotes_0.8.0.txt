0.8.0

New weapon: AF M41 "Pathfinder"
- WW2-style bolt-action sniper rifle, 7.62x54mmR, five-round internal magazine.
- Takes the PU scope, Mosin compensator, improvised suppressor and Mosin bayonet.
- Two finishes: walnut and blued steel (AFM41_Pathfinder), gold floral on black (AFM41_Pathfinder_Gold).
- Uses the Mosin 91/30 animations and sounds.
- First release of this rifle: bolt movement, hand positions and attachment placement are untested in game.

AF-7 Vanguard

Server owners
