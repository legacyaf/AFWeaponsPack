# Allied Forces Weapons Pack 0.12.0
Two new weapons: the **AF StG 44** and the **AF M107A1**.

## AF StG 44
- Assault rifle in the pattern of 1944: stamped steel receiver, beech stock, long curved magazine.
- **7.62x39mm** (vanilla rounds) from its own 30-round magazine. Single shots or full auto, about 570 rounds a minute.
- A third less recoil than the AKM.
- **Attachments:** its own AF ZF4 scope (1.5x), AK muzzle devices, improvised suppressor, ghillie wrap.
- **Two finishes:** beech and steel, or gold floral on black.
- Uses the AKM animations and sounds.
- New finish: baked surface detail, wear where hands and sling rub, pressed-steel flutes, welds and markings, real beech grain, steel shine matched to the vanilla AKM.

## AF M107A1
- **.50 BMG** anti-materiel rifle: semi-automatic, 10-round magazine. Heavy (12.5 kg), long and loud.
- Fires its own .50 BMG rounds from its own steel magazine.
- **Built-in 6-12x scope:** red reticle, zoom readout and a live rangefinder. Zeroing 100 to 1,200 m. The scope can't be taken off.
- Round handguard, full-length rail, ribbed cheek plates, two-chamber muzzle brake, fluted barrel.
- Worn dark Cerakote finish with rivets and its own roll mark.
- Uses the SVD animations, with a little more recoil.

## Server owners
- **New class names:** `AF_StG44`, `AF_StG44_Gold`, `Mag_AFStG44_30Rnd`, `AF_ZF4Optic`, `AF_M107A1`, `Mag_AFM107_10Rnd`, `Ammo_AF50BMG`
- **Merge the loot files in ServerFiles again, or the new items won't spawn.** Neither rifle takes another magazine, and the M107A1 fires no vanilla round.
- The M107A1 is very rare by default: 2 on the map, Military, Tier 4.
- `AF_WeaponsPack.json` adds both weapons by itself on the next start.
- No changes for the AF-7, M41, MP40 or P08.

**Known issues:** the M107A1's scope view and aim height are still being tuned, and its loose rounds use the 7.62 model for now.
