# Allied Forces Weapons Pack 0.13.0: quality pass on every weapon
Every weapon has been rebuilt to a higher standard.

**Cleaner models**
- Clean, hand-laid topology on every weapon. Slots, ports and magazine wells are part of the model now, so surfaces shade smoothly with no streaks.

**Sharper textures**
- AF M107A1: four 4096 texture sheets (was one 2048), with 4096 for its magazine and scope.
- Every weapon rebaked at 4096 from a far more detailed version of its model.

**Finishes that look real**
- Wear where the weapon is handled, grime in the corners, edges worn to bare metal, steel shine matched to vanilla.
- AF-7: near-black anodising, speckled normal map fixed. M41, MP40, P08: proper blued steel. M107A1: darker, even Cerakote.

**AF M107A1 detail**
- Round slotted handguard, A2-style grip, recessed receiver panels, machined bolt carrier, sight knobs, hex screws, QD sling cups, new charging handle and safety.

**AF StG 44**
- Darker blued steel that matches the AKM, a detailed receiver top (welded seam, swages, inspection stamps), the front sight no longer blocks the scope view, and the ZF4 reticle fills the view.

**Server owners:** no class name, loot or settings changes. Update and restart.
