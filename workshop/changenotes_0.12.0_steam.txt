[h1]0.12.0: Two new weapons, the AF StG 44 and the AF M107A1[/h1]

[h1]New weapon: AF StG 44[/h1]
[list]
[*]Assault rifle in the pattern of 1944: stamped steel receiver, beech stock, long curved magazine.
[*][b]7.62x39mm[/b] (the vanilla rounds) from its own 30-round magazine (a new item). Single shots or automatic, at a steady rate of about 570 rounds a minute.
[*]A third less recoil than the AKM.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] its own AF ZF4 scope (a new item, 1.5x, fits no other weapon), AK muzzle devices, the improvised suppressor and a ghillie wrap.
[*][b]Two finishes:[/b] beech and steel, or gold floral on black.
[*]Uses the AKM animations and sounds.
[/list]

Built with a new finishing method:
[list]
[*]Surface detail baked from a far more detailed model of the rifle.
[*]Wear placed where hands, sling and kit really rub.
[*]Pressed-steel detail: flutes, spot welds and markings.
[*]Real beech grain.
[*]Steel shine matched to the vanilla AKM.
[/list]
Checked in game in noon sun, overcast, evening and morning light.

[h1]New weapon: AF M107A1[/h1]
[list]
[*][b].50 BMG[/b] anti-materiel rifle: recoil-operated, semi-automatic, 10-round magazine. Heavy (12.5 kg), long and loud.
[*]Fires its own .50 BMG rounds (a new item) from its own 10-round steel magazine (a new item).
[*][b]Built-in 6-12x scope:[/b] illuminated red reticle, zoom readout and a live rangefinder. The view through it is a round picture, black around the edges. Zeroing from 100 to 1,200 m.
[*]The scope is part of the rifle and cannot be taken off.
[*]Round handguard with lightening holes, full-length top rail, ribbed cheek plates, two-chamber muzzle brake and fluted barrel.
[*]Dark Cerakote finish with edge wear, grime in the corners, rivets, swage lines and its own roll mark.
[*]Uses the SVD animations, with a little more recoil than the SVD.
[/list]
