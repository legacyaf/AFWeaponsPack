0.10.0

New weapon: AF MP40
- WW2-style 9 mm submachine gun: steel tube receiver, bakelite furniture, under-folding stock.
- Fires 9x19mm from its own 32-round magazine (a new item). Automatic only, slow and steady.
- Takes a pistol suppressor and a ghillie wrap.
- Two finishes: bakelite and steel, or gold floral on black.
- Uses the UMP-45 animations.

All weapons: edge and detail pass
- Rounded edges instead of flat chamfers, with smooth shading across them.
- Rounder pins, screws, knobs and barrels; curved grips, trigger guards and triggers.
- Nine levels of detail instead of seven. Up close the weapons have about twice the detail; at range they are lighter than before, to keep frame rate where it was.

Server owners
- New class names: AF_MP40, AF_MP40_Gold, Mag_AFMP40_32Rnd.
- The loot files in ServerFiles have entries for all three. Merge them again, or the MP40 and its magazine will not spawn. No other magazine fits the gun.
- No class name or loot changes for the AF-7 or the M41.
