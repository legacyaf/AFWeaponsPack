## Look

Slim tapered barrel with a front sight band and blade, chamber ring, receiver fork with the breechblock and extractor in it, a flat toggle with knurled round knobs on both sides, V-notch rear sight on the rear toggle link, frame ears carrying the toggle axle, round trigger guard, steeply raked grip with checkered walnut panels. The trigger, take-down lever, safety lever and magazine release are straw-coloured, as on early pistols of this pattern. The orange AF logo is on both grip panels.

## Bones

Read from the skeleton stored in the vanilla model: `magazine`, `trigger` and `recoil` have no parent; `slide`, `hammer`, `bolt`, `slide_catch` and `bullet` are children of `recoil`. On the vanilla model the slide is on `bolt` (990 triangles) and the frame on `recoil`; `slide` carries nothing.

| Bone | Part |
|---|---|
| `recoil` | every part without a bone of its own (`ex.BODY_BONE`), and both suppressor proxies |
| `trigger` | trigger |
| `bolt` | breechblock and extractor |
| `magazine` | the magazine proxy |
| `slide`, `hammer`, `slide_catch`, `bullet` | nothing (the P08 has no hammer) |

## Known compromises

All three come from borrowing the P1's animations.

- **The toggle does not fold up.** The vanilla animation slides the bone `bolt` straight back. Only the breechblock is on it, so the breech opens and the block runs back under a toggle that stays flat. Putting the toggle on the bone as well would push it 4-5 cm out of the back of the pistol.
- **The grip is raked 27 degrees, not 35.** The hand is posed for the P1's 14-16 degrees; at 27 the bottom of the grip is about 1 cm from where the little finger expects it.
- **The magazine leaves the grip along the P1's 13 degree line.** It passes through the front of the grip for a few frames during a reload.

If the P1's slide travel turns out to be more than about 6 cm, the breechblock will show behind the frame when the action is open. The block was kept short (35 mm) to leave that margin; the in-game test will tell.

## Magazine

`Mag_AFP08_8Rnd` is the only magazine the pistol takes. Its model is built around the pistol's magazine proxy (y = 0.0213, z = 0), raked like the grip, with a wooden base plug and its two round knobs below the grip. The pistol keeps the vanilla proxy path `magazine_p1`.

## Status (0.11.0)

Checked outside the game: `config.cpp` compiles, Object Builder's script engine loads all fourteen LODs of both models with 0.87 kg and 0.08 kg mass and convex collision, the mod builds and the signature verifies.
