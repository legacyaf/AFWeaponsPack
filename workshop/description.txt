[h1]Allied Forces Weapons Pack[/h1]

Original weapons made by Allied Forces for DayZ.

[b]Very early stages.[/b] This mod is new and still being tested. Expect rough edges, missing features and frequent updates.

[h1]AF-7 "Vanguard"[/h1]
An original Allied Forces assault rifle with its own model, textures and markings.

[list]
[*][b]Multi-calibre:[/b] feeds 5.56x45mm from STANAG and CMAG magazines, or .308 Winchester from LAR magazines. The magazine you load decides what it fires.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] optic, flashlight and suppressor.
[*][b]Two finishes:[/b] standard (dark receiver, tan furniture) and gold floral on black.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the M4 animations and sounds, with slightly less recoil and sway.
[*]Long slotted handguard, full-length top rail, skeleton stock and hex muzzle brake.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AF7_Vanguard[/b] and [b]AF7_Vanguard_Gold[/b]

[h1]AF M41 "Pathfinder"[/h1]
A WW2-style bolt-action sniper rifle with its own model and textures.

[list]
[*][b]7.62x54mmR[/b], five-round internal magazine, loads from loose rounds or stripper clips.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] PU scope, ghillie wrap, Mosin compensator, improvised suppressor and Mosin bayonet.
[*][b]Two finishes:[/b] walnut and blued steel, or gold floral on black.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the Mosin 91/30 animations and sounds.
[*]Full-length walnut stock, turned-down bolt, tangent rear sight, cleaning rod and an AF unit disc in the butt.
[*]Arsenal stamps and serial numbers on the receiver and stock, handling wear on the wood and worn bluing on the steel.
[*]Rounds are visible in the magazine when the bolt is open.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AFM41_Pathfinder[/b] and [b]AFM41_Pathfinder_Gold[/b]

[h1]AF MP40[/h1]
A WW2-style 9 mm submachine gun with its own model, textures and magazine.

[list]
[*][b]9x19mm[/b] from its own 32-round stick magazine. Automatic only, at a slow and steady rate.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] pistol suppressor and ghillie wrap.
[*][b]Two finishes:[/b] bakelite and steel, or gold floral on black.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the UMP-45 animations.
[*]Steel tube receiver, bakelite frame and grip, resting bar under the barrel, hooded front sight and an under-folding stock.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AF_MP40[/b], [b]AF_MP40_Gold[/b] and the magazine [b]Mag_AFMP40_32Rnd[/b]

[h1]AF P08[/h1]
A toggle-lock 9 mm service pistol with its own model, textures and magazine.

[list]
[*][b]9x19mm[/b] from its own 8-round magazine.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] pistol suppressor and improvised suppressor.
[*][b]Two finishes:[/b] walnut grips and blued steel, or gold floral grips on black.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the P1 animations and sounds.
[*]Slim tapered barrel, knurled toggle knobs, straw-coloured small parts and a raked grip with checkered walnut panels.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AF_P08[/b], [b]AF_P08_Gold[/b] and the magazine [b]Mag_AFP08_8Rnd[/b]

[h1]AF StG 44[/h1]
An assault rifle in the pattern of 1944, with its own model, textures, magazine and scope.

[list]
[*][b]7.62x39mm[/b] (the vanilla rounds) from its own 30-round curved magazine. Single shots or automatic, at a steady rate.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] its own AF ZF4 scope (1.5x), AK muzzle devices, improvised suppressor and ghillie wrap.
[*][b]Two finishes:[/b] beech and steel, or gold floral on black.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the AKM animations and sounds, with a third less recoil.
[*]Stamped steel receiver with pressed flutes, spot welds and markings, a stamped handguard with cooling holes, and a beech stock.
[*]Wear where hands, sling and kit rub.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AF_StG44[/b], [b]AF_StG44_Gold[/b], the magazine [b]Mag_AFStG44_30Rnd[/b] and the scope [b]AF_ZF4Optic[/b]

[h1]AF M107A1[/h1]
A .50 BMG anti-materiel rifle with its own model, textures, magazine, rounds and scope.

[list]
[*][b].50 BMG[/b], its own rounds, from its own 10-round steel magazine. Recoil-operated and semi-automatic. Heavy (12.5 kg), long and loud.
[*][b]Built-in 6-12x scope:[/b] illuminated red reticle, zoom readout and a live rangefinder, zeroing from 100 to 1,200 m. The scope is part of the rifle and cannot be taken off.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the SVD animations, with a little more recoil than the SVD.
[*]Round handguard with lightening holes, full-length top rail, ribbed cheek plates, a two-chamber muzzle brake and a fluted barrel.
[*]Worn dark Cerakote finish, rivets and its own roll mark.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AF_M107A1[/b], the magazine [b]Mag_AFM107_10Rnd[/b] and the rounds [b]Ammo_AF50BMG[/b]

[h1]AF PeaceKeeper[/h1]
A modern 7.62 battle rifle with its own model, textures and stock.

[list]
[*][b]7.62x51mm (.308 Win)[/b] from the vanilla 20-round SCAR-H magazines. Single shots, 3-round burst or full auto.
[*][b]Live round counter[/b] on the side of the rifle. It glows, so it reads at night.
[*][b]Gold ghost-ring iron sights[/b] with a red front post tip.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] optic, flashlight, improvised suppressor and ghillie wrap.
[*][b]Its own AF stock.[/b] The vanilla SCAR stocks fit the rifle too, and the AF stock fits the vanilla SCAR-H.
[*][b]Handling:[/b] uses the SCAR-H animations and sounds.
[*]Matte black with orange trim, a stippled grip, engraved markings and wear where the rifle is handled.
[/list]

Class names: [b]AF_PeaceKeeper[/b] and the stock [b]AF_PeaceKeeperStock[/b]

[h1]Server settings[/h1]
Server owners tune the pack in one file, [b]AF_WeaponsPack.json[/b], which the server writes into its profiles folder on first start.

[list]
[*][b]Per weapon and for the whole pack:[/b] recoil, sway, damage and jam chance.
[*][b]Per weapon:[/b] switch it off, or add wear per shot.
[*][b]AF Weapon Repair Kit:[/b] repairs the pack's weapons, even ruined ones. Repair time, amount restored and uses per kit are settings.
[*][b]Optional:[/b] weapons take no damage from bullets; a short sound when a weapon is aimed, zoomed or zeroed.
[*]The settings only affect this pack's weapons. Vanilla weapons and other mods are never touched.
[/list]

Every setting is explained in [b]ServerFiles\SETTINGS.md[/b] inside the mod. Class name of the kit: [b]AF_WeaponRepairKit[/b]

[h1]Licensed servers only[/h1]
This mod only works on servers that hold a licence. When a server starts, the mod checks its IP and game port against our whitelist; on any other server the weapons are removed.

[list]
[*][b]Players:[/b] subscribe and join an Allied Forces server or another licensed server. Nothing else to set up.
[*][b]Server owners:[/b] licences are free during early access, one per server. Request one from the Mod Shop on the Allied Forces website: https://legacyaf.github.io/Alliedforces/#modshop
[/list]

[h1]For licensed server owners[/h1]
[list]
[*]Copy [b]Keys\AlliedForces.bikey[/b] to your server's keys folder.
[*]Add the mod to your [b]-mod=[/b] launch line.
[*]Your server must be able to reach the internet when it starts, or the licence check fails and the weapons stay disabled.
[*]The licence is tied to your server's IP and game port. If either changes, ask Allied Forces staff to update it.
[*]Loot files are included in the mod's [b]ServerFiles[/b] folder: types and spawnable types for every weapon, with a README. Gold finishes are listed but set not to spawn by default.
[/list]

[h1]Known issues[/h1]
[list]
[*]Hand position, sight alignment and attachment placement are still being tuned.
[*].308 uses the M4 firing sound, and LAR magazine fit is still being tuned.
[*]The AF-7's safety and bolt catch do not animate yet.
[*]The MP40 and P08 are new: firing, cocking and the first-person view have not been tuned in game yet.
[*]The server settings are new: recoil, sway, switching weapons off and the repair kit have been tested; damage, jam chance and wear settings have not been tried in play yet.
[*]The P08 borrows the P1 animations: its toggle stays flat instead of folding up when the action opens, and the magazine clips through the grip for a moment during a reload.
[*]The M107A1 is new: the view through its scope and the rifle's height when aiming are still being tuned.
[*]The M107A1's loose .50 BMG rounds use the vanilla 7.62 round model for now.
[*]The StG 44's selector and magazine catch do not animate.
[*]The PeaceKeeper is new: its fire modes and round counter have been tested on a local server, not yet on a busy live one.
[/list]
