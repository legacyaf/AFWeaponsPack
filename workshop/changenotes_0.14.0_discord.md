# Allied Forces Weapons Pack 0.14.0: The PeaceKeeper
A new weapon: the **AF PeaceKeeper**, a modern 7.62 battle rifle with a live round counter.

**AF PeaceKeeper**
- 7.62x51mm (.308 Win) from the vanilla 20-round SCAR-H magazines.
- Three fire modes: single shots, 3-round burst and full auto (about 610 rounds a minute).
- Live round counter on the side of the rifle. It glows, so it reads at night.
- Gold ghost-ring iron sights with a red front post tip.
- Its own AF stock. Vanilla SCAR stocks fit too, and the AF stock fits the vanilla SCAR-H.
- Attachments: optic, flashlight, improvised suppressor, ghillie wrap.
- Matte black with orange trim, stippled grip, engraved markings.

**Fixes**
- Server settings now reach every player (they were sent in one message that had grown too long for the game).

**Server owners:** two new loot entries in `ServerFiles` (`AF_PeaceKeeper`, `AF_PeaceKeeperStock`) and one in the spawnable types file. Add them to make the rifle spawn. Nothing else changes; update and restart.
